# Bonds and fixed-income investments — FY 2025–26

Load this module when the user mentions a government security, Treasury Bill,
bond, debenture, market-linked debenture (MLD), coupon, bond redemption, or
bond maturity. The detailed primary-source analysis is in
`docs/research/ay2026-27-bonds-fixed-income.md`.

## Evidence to ask for

Ask for the instrument term sheet or information memorandum, purchase and
sale/redemption/maturity contract notes, demat/RBI Retail Direct statements,
issuer coupon/redemption statements, relevant bank entries, Form 16A, Form
26AS, and AIS/TIS. For every disposal, establish from dated evidence:

- ISIN, legal form, MLD status, and whether it was listed on the event date;
- account-local lot lineage, acquisition date, clean capital cost, and fees;
- event type/date/quantity, clean capital proceeds, transfer expenses, and STT;
- separately evidenced accrued interest and total dirty cash settlement; and
- gross coupon/interest, TDS, net bank receipt, and source provenance.

Do not derive a clean/accrued split from the security name, coupon rate, or a
single dirty total. Ask for the contract note/RBI statement or a human
resolution. STT participates in cash reconciliation but is never deducted in
the capital-gain computation.

## AY 2026–27 routing

- Every MLD sale, redemption, or maturity is deemed short-term under section
  50AA, regardless of acquisition date, holding period, or listing.
- An unlisted bond or unlisted debenture transferred, redeemed, or matured on
  or after 23 July 2024 is deemed short-term under section 50AA. The 1 April
  2023 acquisition cut-off applies to specified mutual funds, not these debt
  instruments.
- Ordinary listed debt is long-term only after more than 12 months.
- An unlisted government/other security outside the bond-or-debenture legal
  form is ordinarily long-term only after more than 24 months.
- A properly notified statutory zero-coupon bond has a 12-month test, but an
  unlisted zero-coupon instrument that may also fall within section 50AA needs
  human legal resolution.
- Periodic coupon and separately evidenced seller accrued interest go to
  income from other sources under the taxpayer's consistently followed
  accounting method; they do not become capital proceeds.

Use account-local FIFO. An own-account transfer carries original date and basis
and is not a sale. Reconcile every coupon/disposal to bank cash and reconcile
claimed TDS to Form 26AS before producing filing values.

## Block instead of guessing

Stop filing-ready computation and ask the human when any of these is unresolved:

- dirty consideration cannot be split into clean capital and accrued interest;
- legal form, MLD status, event-date listing, cost, or lot lineage is missing;
- issuer/broker, bank, Form 16A, Form 26AS, or AIS facts conflict;
- the security is deep-discount, stripped, defaulted, restructured, perpetual,
  converted, written down, or has disputed basis/recovery allocation;
- a statutory-zero-coupon classification collides with section 50AA; or
- the holding is stock-in-trade rather than an investment.

The normalized ledger is the computational source of truth; a broker's final
P&L can support a provisional view but does not replace economic-event/evidence
reconciliation for filing readiness.
