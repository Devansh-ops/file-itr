# AY 2026–27 mutual-fund classification (FY 2025–26)

Research date: 29 July 2026. Scope: Indian capital-gains classification for units transferred in FY 2025–26 by an ordinary resident investor. This note separates the rules supplied by tax law from the scheme facts that must come from official product and transaction evidence.

## Conclusions

1. A fund is **equity-oriented** only if it meets section 112A’s statutory portfolio test: generally at least 65% of total proceeds in listed equity shares of domestic companies; the special fund-of-funds route requires at least 90% in units of another exchange-traded fund which itself has at least 90% in such domestic listed equity. The percentage is the annual average of monthly opening/closing averages. A SEBI label such as “equity”, “hybrid”, “arbitrage”, “FoF”, or “ETF” is not itself the tax test. [Income-tax Act, section 112A](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-112a-60)
2. For AY 2026–27, section 50AA’s amended **specified mutual fund** definition covers (a) a fund investing **more than 65%** of total proceeds in debt and money-market instruments, or (b) a fund investing **65% or more** in units of a fund in (a). These percentages use the annual average of **daily closing** figures. For units of such a fund acquired on or after 1 April 2023, the gain is deemed short-term regardless of holding period. [Income-tax Act, section 50AA](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-50aa-4)
3. The new section 50AA definition was substituted by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 with effect from **1 April 2026**, so it applies to **AY 2026–27 / FY 2025–26**. Up to AY 2025–26 the definition instead looked for not more than 35% in domestic-company equity. The Income Tax Department’s current capital-gains guide states both cutovers. [Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024](https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Documents/Finance-No.2-Act-2024.pdf), [Income Tax Department capital-gains guide](https://wmstatic-prd.incometaxindia.gov.in/web/guest/w/capital-gain)
4. A non-equity fund which is **not** a specified mutual fund is no longer automatically deemed short-term. For FY 2025–26, a listed unit is long-term after 12 months and an unlisted “other unit” after 24 months; otherwise its short-term gain is taxed at ordinary applicable rates, while long-term gain is taxed under section 112 at 12.5% without indexation. [Income Tax Department holding-period and rate table](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/en/sale-of-shares)

## Statutory decision table

All holding periods below are measured lot by lot and mean “long-term only if held for **more than** the stated period.”

| Product facts for the relevant year | Acquisition / holding rule | FY 2025–26 tax bucket |
|---|---|---|
| Section 112A equity-oriented fund; STT condition met on transfer | 12 months, listed or unlisted | Up to 12 months: section 111A STCG at 20%. Over 12 months: section 112A LTCG at 12.5% on the aggregate section 112A gain above ₹1.25 lakh. [Sections 111A](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-111a) and [112A](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-112a-60) |
| Section 50AA specified mutual fund; lot acquired on or after 1 April 2023 | Holding period is overridden | Deemed STCG at the investor’s applicable ordinary rate. |
| Same debt-heavy fund, but lot acquired before 1 April 2023 | Listed: 12 months; unlisted: 24 months | Ordinary STCG if not over the threshold; otherwise section 112 LTCG at 12.5% without indexation. |
| Neither equity-oriented nor specified | Listed: 12 months; unlisted: 24 months | Ordinary STCG if not over the threshold; otherwise section 112 LTCG at 12.5% without indexation. |

The Income Tax Department publishes the same 20% / 12.5% special rates and 12/24-month split, and SBI Mutual Fund’s FY 2025–26 first-party tax reckoner applies the acquisition-date split for debt-heavy funds. [Income Tax Department special-rate table](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/computation-of-tax-for-individual-1), [SBI Mutual Fund FY 2025–26 tax reckoner](https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/pdf/sbi-mf-tax-reckoner-fy-2025-26.pdf)

Equity-oriented status and STT eligibility are separate facts: failure to evidence an STT-chargeable transfer removes the section 111A/112A special-rate route, but does not turn the scheme into a debt or residual fund for holding-period purposes.

### Product-type implications

These are decision rules, not classification by name:

- **Indian-equity funds:** equity-oriented only when the section 112A domestic-listed-equity test and STT condition are evidenced.
- **Debt funds:** normally specified under the AY 2026–27 definition when the annual daily-average debt/money-market exposure is more than 65%. Exactly 65% does **not** satisfy the direct-fund limb (“more than”).
- **Hybrid funds:** classify from the actual statutory ratios. A conservative hybrid may be specified; an aggressive hybrid may be equity-oriented; a balanced or dynamic fund may be neither. The scheme category alone cannot decide it.
- **Arbitrage and equity-savings funds:** SEBI’s FY 2025–26 categorisation required at least 65% in “equity and equity-related instruments,” but section 112A requires listed equity shares of domestic companies and a particular annual-average calculation. Treat as equity-oriented only with the AMC’s tax classification or evidence that proves the statutory test. [SEBI categorisation circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2017/1509103633344.pdf)
- **Fund of funds:** a domestic equity-ETF FoF is equity-oriented only if the 90%/90% section 112A route is met. A debt FoF is specified if at least 65% is invested in direct funds that themselves satisfy section 50AA(a). Other FoFs—such as overseas-equity, gold, or mixed-asset FoFs—are ordinarily in the residual “neither” bucket unless their actual facts meet one of the statutory tests.
- **ETFs:** the wrapper does not determine tax. A domestic-equity ETF may be equity-oriented; a debt ETF may be specified; gold/silver or foreign-equity ETFs are generally neither. Listing normally gives a non-equity/non-specified ETF the 12-month holding threshold. SEBI’s scheme rules distinguish index funds/ETFs by the tracked index, not by one tax class. [SEBI FY 2025–26 Master Circular landing page](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/jun-2024/master-circular-for-mutual-funds_84441.html)

AMFI also records that the amended section 50AA definition applies from FY 2025–26 and narrows the provision to debt-oriented schemes, but its general tax page is corroboration rather than scheme-specific proof. [AMFI tax regime for mutual funds](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=TaxRegimeForMutualFunds)

## Acquisition dates, FIFO, switches, and consolidations

- Treat each purchase, SIP instalment, dividend-reinvestment allotment, and switch-in as its own acquisition lot. The official SFT instructions require FIFO matching of each mutual-fund debit to corresponding credits and use the holding period to classify each disposal. [Income Tax Department SFT filing guide](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/en/statement-of-financial-transaction-sft)
- For long-term equity-oriented units acquired before 1 February 2018, preserve the actual acquisition date and the 31 January 2018 fair-market-value inputs needed for section 55’s grandfathered cost rule; do not replace the lot with a 2018 acquisition. [Income-tax Act, section 55](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-55-63)
- A normal switch is two events: SEBI treats the **switch-out as a redemption** and the **switch-in as a purchase**. The switch-out therefore realises a disposal, and the destination units receive a new acquisition/allotment date (and are tested separately against section 50AA’s 1 April 2023 cut-off). [SEBI switch-transaction guidance](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/docfiles/9985_t.html) This also follows the Act’s definition of “transfer”, which includes sale, exchange, relinquishment, or extinguishment of rights. [Income-tax Act, section 2(47)](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-2-54)
- Do not confuse a voluntary switch with a qualifying scheme/plan consolidation. Section 47(xviii)/(xix) can make a qualifying consolidation non-taxable; the successor units carry the predecessor holding period and cost under section 2(42A) and section 49. [Section 47](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-47-63), [section 49](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-49-66)

## What the law determines vs. what evidence must supply

### Law determines

- the 65%, 90%/90%, and debt-FoF thresholds and their averaging methods;
- the 1 April 2023 acquisition cut-off in section 50AA;
- the 12-month versus 24-month holding periods;
- the need for STT for sections 111A/112A;
- the 20%, 12.5%, ₹1.25 lakh, and ordinary-rate buckets; and
- which reorganisations are non-transfers and carry holding period/cost.

### Product and transaction evidence must supply

For every scheme/ISIN and disposal, retain:

1. **Identity and transactions:** AMC/RTA statement or CAS for scheme name, plan/option, folio, transaction type, dates, units, NAV/amount, and exit load; depository/broker contract evidence for ETF exchange trades. AMFI describes CAS as the combined record of transactions across mutual funds and demat accounts. [AMFI CAS facility](https://www.amfiindia.com/online-center/download-cas)
2. **Reconciliation:** AIS/SFT records for the reported debit, estimated consideration/cost, security class, and FIFO result. AIS displays information received from SFT reporting entities, and the official SFT guide expressly says the taxpayer may modify estimated sale consideration and cost; ETFs and exchange-based transactions may be absent from the mutual-fund SFT file. AIS/SFT is therefore a reconciliation source, not conclusive proof. [AIS FAQ](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/ais-faq), [SFT guide](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/en/statement-of-financial-transaction-sft)
3. **Tax class for FY 2025–26:** preferably an AMC-issued scheme-wise tax classification/certificate or FY 2025–26 tax annexure stating equity-oriented, specified, or neither and its effective period.
4. **Underlying facts:** the applicable SID/KIM plus addenda for scheme category, investment mandate, underlying index/funds, and listing; AMC/AMFI monthly portfolios and annual report for actual holdings. SEBI requires scheme portfolio disclosure through AMC/AMFI channels, but a public month-end portfolio cannot by itself prove section 50AA’s **annual average of daily closings**. Obtain an AMC classification/certificate where the result depends on that calculation.
5. **STT and listing:** redemption statement/contract note showing STT and exchange/listing evidence for the relevant unit.

SEBI/AMFI categories are useful corroboration and discovery metadata, but must not override the Act’s different asset definitions and averaging formulas. If the official AMC classification is unavailable, conflicts across sources, changes mid-year, or sits close to a statutory threshold, classification should remain unresolved and block filing-ready output until the AMC or a human supplies authoritative evidence.

## Minimum normalized classification record

Store, per scheme and FY: canonical scheme name, ISIN and AMFI scheme code; AMC; direct/regular and option; listed/unlisted; statutory class (`equity_oriented`, `specified_mutual_fund`, or `other`); classification period; direct/ FoF test used; portfolio percentage and averaging method; STT status; source document URL/file and date; acquisition lot date; and any unresolved conflict. Preserve the raw evidence alongside the normalized value.
